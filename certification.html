Hanson Mathematics Certification

Hanson Mathematics is developing a rigorous examination-based certification program designed to provide an additional way for individuals to demonstrate mathematical competency.

Demonstrate What You Know

Traditional educational credentials can provide useful information about a candidate's background. However, they do not always provide employers with a direct measure of a candidate's current mathematical abilities.

The Hanson Mathematics Certification program is being developed to provide candidates with an opportunity to demonstrate mathematical competency through standardized examinations.

The goal is simple: provide a rigorous and transparent way to demonstrate mathematical knowledge independent of where or how that knowledge was acquired.

For Candidates

Candidates will be able to demonstrate competency in defined areas of mathematics through examination.

Future certifications may assess areas such as:

  • Algebra and quantitative reasoning
  • Calculus
  • Linear algebra
  • Differential equations
  • Probability and statistics
  • Mathematical modeling
  • Proof writing and advanced mathematical reasoning

Successful candidates will receive a verifiable credential documenting the mathematical competencies they have demonstrated.

For Employers

The program is also being designed as a skills-based hiring resource for employers seeking candidates with strong mathematical abilities.

Potential employer services may include:

  • Verification of Hanson Mathematics credentials
  • Standardized mathematical competency assessments
  • Custom assessments based on job-specific mathematical requirements
  • Identification of candidates who have demonstrated specified mathematical skills

The objective is to give employers an additional method of evaluating mathematical ability beyond educational background alone.

Program Development

The Hanson Mathematics Certification program is currently in development. Examination standards, competency frameworks, scoring procedures, credential verification, and employer participation are being developed before the program is formally launched.

Individuals and organizations interested in the development of the program are welcome to contact Hanson Mathematics.
